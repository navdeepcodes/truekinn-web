# Child Safety and CSAE Policy – Truekinn

Truekinn is a private, college-focused community platform intended only for users aged 18 and above.

## Zero Tolerance Policy
Truekinn has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM), child sexual exploitation, or any content involving minors in a sexual context.

## Age Restriction
- Truekinn is intended strictly for users **18 years and older**
- Accounts found to belong to minors are removed immediately

## Content Moderation
We actively moderate content using:
- Automated content filtering
- User reporting tools
- Manual review by administrators

Any content that violates our policies is removed promptly.

## Reporting
Users can report content or accounts directly within the app.
Reports related to child safety are treated with highest priority.

## Cooperation With Authorities
Truekinn cooperates fully with law enforcement agencies and will report any confirmed CSAM or exploitation in accordance with applicable laws.
